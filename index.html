READ BEFORE INTERPRETING
ASSESSMENT STANDARD

Evidence first. Estimates labeled.

DEFENCE TECH-INSIGHT is an independent research prototype. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government, armed forces or defence organization.

01
Define the capability

Use a specific, observable capability rather than a broad sector label.

02
Collect attributable evidence

Prefer primary government, audit, parliamentary, academic and patent sources. Record source title, publication date, scope and URL.

03
Make like-for-like comparisons

Document definitions, time period and uncertainty. Different public disclosure levels do not establish a capability gap.

04
Separate facts from interpretation

Label verified facts, analytical judgments, estimates and illustrative examples distinctly. Do not invent statistics.
